## Press Release

**Tysons, Virginia**, April 18, 2024 — **Daybreak LLC**, today announced a strategic partnership with DataRobot, providing customers with custom AI/ML solutions powered by DataRobot’s robust and intuitive platform.

Daybreak leverages our AI experts to tackle geospatial and data science challenges, solving national problems.  We bring value driven AI to federal Defense, intelligence, and civilian agencies.  

Daybreak’s Chief Technology Officer, Jason Dalton, said “Daybreak is excited to expand on its core mission of bringing value-driven AI to help solve critical national challenges, by adding DataRobot’s AI Platform to our toolkit.  DataRobot’s AI Platform can dramatically reduce the cost and time to mission for bringing AI and ML to solve an array of problems and make better operational decisions. Daybreak brings applied AI experts with deep domain expertise to make this happen.”

The DataRobot AI Platform is the only complete AI lifecycle platform that interoperates with an organization’s existing investments in data, applications and business processes, and can be deployed on any cloud environment. Global organizations, including 40% of the Fortune 50, rely on DataRobot to drive greater impact and value from AI. 

Contact us now to discuss how we can help transform the future of your agency.
